Add AASHE STARS dropdowns

Upload a food purchasing worksheet and we'll email it back with dropdown columns for the STARS sustainable and ethical categories. Fill them in, then upload the completed sheet to get your results with the STARS shares.

Note: This process is experimental. It has not been reviewed and approved for use as part of compliance with an AASHE/STARS values-based food procurement analysis. One major difference is that it typically measures relative to weights and/or calories purchased rather than expenditures.

Your worksheet is on its way

  1. Look for an email titled “Your worksheet with AASHE STARS dropdowns”, usually within a few minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.
  2. Open the attached Excel file, fill in the dropdowns that apply, and save it.
  3. Upload the saved file at Food Impact Analysis to get your results.

What gets added

  • Six AASHE columns: Organic, Animal Welfare, Environment, Labor, Small Producers and Seafood. Each lists the certifications and programs STARS 3.0 accepts in that category.
  • Local Credit and Social Impact Supplier (y or blank). STARS gives no credit for local purchases alone; local is tracked only.
  • Plant Based (y or a processing category 1–4) and Explanation.

A Notes sheet explains each column, and an AASHE Lookup sheet lists the STARS categories each option meets.

Then

  1. Fill in the dropdowns that apply. Leave the rest blank.
  2. Upload the completed sheet at Food Impact Analysis.
  3. Your results include the STARS sustainable/ethical and plant-based shares, with each product counted once.
